      About the data. Dates and registration windows were checked on November 26, 2026 against race sites, organizer announcements and race calendars. Dates marked est. are projected from the race's usual weekend or last year's timing; confirm them on the race site before you book.

      Temperatures are typical race-morning and afternoon highs for that place and week, based on climate normals. They are not forecasts. Each race card links to the live forecast, which becomes useful about a week out.

      Coverage. Full marathons: every US and Canadian marathon on FindMyMarathon's calendar, with its course profile and last-edition finisher count. Halves: the large halves from RunSignup's Top 100 list, plus complete New England lists from running.life; other states are still being added. Races whose next date was not yet posted are projected one year from the last edition and marked est. Imported races link to a web search instead of a verified race site.

      Popularity combines field size (RunSignup's 2025 Top 100 race-weekend totals, or marathon finishers) with demand: lotteries, qualifier-only entry and fast sell-outs add a level. Demand notes come from organizer and news reports; races without a report say "Demand not tracked".

      Course difficulty is rated 1 (flat and fast) to 5 (major climbs or exposure), based on each course's published profile, its elevation and known hills, wind or heat.
