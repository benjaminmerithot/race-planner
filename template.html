          About the data. Hand-picked races were checked on November 26, 2026 against race sites, organizer announcements and race calendars. Imported listings were last refreshed on {{BUILT}}. Dates marked est. are projected from the race's usual weekend or last year's timing; confirm them on the race site before you book.

          Temperatures are typical race-morning and afternoon highs for that place and week, based on climate normals. For imported races they are NOAA's 1991 to 2020 daily climate normals at the nearest weather station of similar elevation, averaged over race week. Where no station is close, they are the average daily low and high for that week from 2016 to 2025, from Open-Meteo historical weather data (CC BY 4.0). They are not forecasts. Each race card links to the live forecast, which becomes useful about a week out.

          Coverage. US full and half marathons listed on RunSignup for the next 12 months, every US and Canadian marathon on FindMyMarathon's calendar (with its course profile and last-edition finisher count), and New England halves from running.life. Canadian halves are not covered yet. Races whose next date was not yet posted are projected one year from the last edition and marked est. Races found on RunSignup link to their own site or RunSignup page; the rest link to a web search.

          On the map, pin colour shows the longest distance offered and bigger pins are more popular races. In the list, click a row to find it on the map.

          Popularity combines field size (RunSignup's 2025 Top 100 race-weekend totals, or marathon finishers) with demand: lotteries, qualifier-only entry and fast sell-outs add a level. Demand notes come from organizer and news reports; races without a report say "Demand not tracked".

          Course difficulty is rated 1 (flat and fast) to 5 (major climbs or exposure), based on each course's published profile, its elevation and known hills, wind or heat.
